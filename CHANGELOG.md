# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-16

Initial public release: offline recorded wood and traditional gas, plus a modern wide burner composite with moving recorded flame emission. Five photographed surrounds, frame width and frameless mode, linear-only flame height, burn speed, color, lighting, ash, optional synthesized crackle and slow drift are configurable from the remote.

Includes a native DreamService, validated settings provider, bundled offline credits and a checksum-verifying installer. The final production APK was checked on Google TV API 34 and physical AFTDEC012E/API 30 for remote settings and preview, modern linear playback, frameless blue small/slow bound test-Dream, Back and exact state preservation. Automatic idle behavior across vendors, native 4K and long-term power/thermal results remain outside the verified scope.
